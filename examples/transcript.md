## Spaghetti with Anchovy Breadcrumbs

Salty, crunchy, and done in the time the water takes to boil.

Serves 2 · Prep 5 min · Cook 15 min

### Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 6 anchovy fillets, in oil
- 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 60 g coarse breadcrumbs
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 handful flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Salt, to taste

### Instructions

1. **Boil the pasta** Salt the water heavily and cook the spaghetti for 9 minutes, until just short of al dente.
2. **Toast the crumbs** Meanwhile, warm 2 tbsp oil in a wide pan and toast the breadcrumbs for 4 minutes until deep gold. Tip onto a plate.
3. **Melt the anchovies** Add the rest of the oil, the garlic and the anchovies to the pan and cook gently for 3 minutes until the fillets dissolve.
4. **Toss** Drain the pasta, keeping a mug of water. Toss it through the anchovy oil with a splash of the water until glossy.
5. **Finish** Off the heat, fold through the parsley and half the crumbs. Serve with the rest scattered on top.

### Notes

- Do not brown the garlic — bitter garlic ruins the whole plate.
- Stale sourdough blitzed in a processor beats shop-bought crumbs every time.
