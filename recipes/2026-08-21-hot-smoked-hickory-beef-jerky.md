# Hot Smoked Hickory Beef Jerky — Weber Q + Smokai

Smoked start to finish on the Weber Q with the Smokai 1L Classic running
continuous hickory. No dehydrator — the Q's burners hold 70–80°C at the grate
while the generator does the smoke.
