# Pad See Ew - Thai Stir Fried Noodles

Wide rice noodles stir-fried with chicken, egg and Chinese broccoli in a dark,
smoky soy sauce. Cook the chicken separately from the noodles — home stoves
can't match restaurant burners, so splitting the two is what gets real
caramelisation on the noodles instead of a stewed result.
